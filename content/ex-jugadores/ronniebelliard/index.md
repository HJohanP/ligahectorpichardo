- Milwaukee Brewers (1998-2002) 🇺🇸
- Colorado Rockies (2003) 🇺🇸
- Cleveland Indians (2004-2006) 🇺🇸
- St. Louis Cardinals (2006) 🇺🇸
- Washington Nationals (2007-2009) 🇺🇸
- Los Angeles Dodgers (2009-2010) 🇺🇸
